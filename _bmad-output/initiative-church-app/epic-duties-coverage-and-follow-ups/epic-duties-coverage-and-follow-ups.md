# Assigned duties, coverage and accountable follow-up

## Description

Implement Duties as the sole assignment authority and Follow-ups as the shared all-owner task registry. Deliver department rotas, revision-specific member/direct responses, coverage and practical contact work, while exposing owner operations for cell programmes and later care/reporting/custody consumers.

Milestone: 2.

## Outcome

Members know their current responsibilities, and leaders can see each uncovered or unanswered position and its accountable next action without a disconnected tracker.

## Requirements

The requirement source is the parent spec, CAP-5, CAP-6, read with every companion and with the owner's Q2 decisions in `owner-decisions-milestone-2.md`, which replace the Q2 proposals where they differ. The local ids below hold this epic's contribution; each maps to the parent capability shown, and the spec, its companions and the architecture stay authoritative for detail.

- **D1** (CAP-5) — Admin creates departments, gives each a leader and an optional deputy as Duties-registered Identity scopes, and adds approved members, including members without a login. Every duty occurrence has exactly one owning department or cell, only that scope's authorised leaders manage it, Pastor has church-wide oversight and Admin can manage; a cell leader gains no department authority. Source: functional-requirements.md Set up departments and duties, Roles and permissions; AD-1, AD-4, AD-6.
- **D2** (CAP-5) — Leaders create one-off and recurring occurrences in church time (Duties owns standalone duty recurrence) with one slot per required position: duty, date, reporting/start/end time, location, optional linked event, instructions and optional topic/scripture. Drafts are visible only to the managing leader, deputy and authorised Pastor/Admin; publishing creates the assignments and their notifications. Series edits offer this occurrence or this and future, preserve the past, and occurrence-wide edits or cancellation update all positions together. Source: functional-requirements.md Create and publish a rota, Core data model (Duties and owning scope); AD-6, AD-9; design-contract.md Rota.
- **D3** (CAP-5) — A slot has at most one current assignment. A material change (date, reporting/start/end time, location, duty or assignee) creates a new pending assignment revision with a recalculated deadline, even for the same person, and preserves the old response, actor, time and provenance; a notes-only edit sends an update without resetting acceptance. Only the current revision counts toward coverage, and a response to an old revision is refused with what changed. Source: functional-requirements.md Changes and completion, Core data model (Assignments); AD-2, AD-6; AC-10.
- **D4** (CAP-5) — My duties shows Needs a response, Upcoming and Past across every department and cell part, with detail, Accept or Can't make it with an optional note, Can't make it anymore after acceptance, bulk acceptance of selected listed dates after confirmation, Not sent until the server confirms, Add to calendar with a stale-export caveat, a deep link back and the Home My duties card. Source: functional-requirements.md My duties, Navigation and visual style (Member Home); design-contract.md My duties and detail, State fidelity 5; AC-08.
- **D5** (CAP-5) — Overlapping time ranges across departments and cell-programme assignments raise a warning that discloses only what the viewer may see, the candidate list shows each person's upcoming load, and a leader may override with a recorded reason while the assignee still chooses whether to accept. Availability is not assumed. Source: functional-requirements.md Changes and completion; design-contract.md Leader coverage, State fidelity 7; AD-6.
- **D6** (CAP-5, CAP-7) — Each duty has a response deadline set by its creator, or the owner's lead-time default when none is set, never later than the start; a deadline already passed marks the assignment Short notice and asks for a response now. Reminders are configured per duty by its creator, with the owner defaults when none are set; response reminders stop on accept or decline, accepted duties keep their pre-service reminders, the leader is alerted when a deadline passes, and cancellation, reassignment, deactivation or removal stops the member's pending reminders. Held or accountless assignees route to the leader's Needs contact list. There are no quiet hours. Source: owner-decisions-milestone-2.md; functional-requirements.md Proposed duty reminder defaults (as superseded by the owner decision); AD-8, AD-9; AC-09, AC-11.
- **D7** (CAP-6) — A scoped coverage queue (next seven days by default, with date and duty filters) shows Unfilled, Awaiting response with deadline, Overdue response, Declined or changed, Confirmed against required, and Needs contact. For each issue the leader opens the assignment, contacts the member through permitted routes, records a practical contact attempt and next follow-up date, records Confirmed by leader with actor, time and reason, reassigns or cancels. Leader Home shows Needs attention; Pastor sees authorised gaps church-wide without unrelated private notes. Source: functional-requirements.md Leader follow-up and coverage; design-contract.md Leader coverage, Rota, Home; AC-03, AC-08, AC-09.
- **D8** (CAP-6) — Follow-ups owns one task per source_type/source_id/purpose with one accountable owner, a supervisor and scope, a next action, the original due date, Open/In progress/Waiting (with review date)/Done/Closed (with reason), outcomes, activity and separately restricted private notes. Every owner sees My follow-ups on mobile Home and in staff web; owners act on their own tasks, leaders and authorised Admins reassign; Admin sees routing metadata only; a supervisor records updates with attribution for an owner without online access. Source owners register purposes and legal lifecycle effects. Task reminders fire on assignment and at the actionable deadline, the supervisor is told once a day later, reassignment moves future reminders to the new owner and closure cancels them. Source: functional-requirements.md Follow-up tasks; AD-7; AC-12, AC-18.
- **D9** (CAP-5) — After the duty the leader records Completed, Excused or No-show with an optional note and a correction path; passing time never means a no-show, and response, coverage and completion stay separate. Member assignment histories support fair workload discussions and are not public rankings. Source: functional-requirements.md Changes and completion, Leader follow-up and coverage; design-contract.md State fidelity 6.
- **D10** (CAP-5, CAP-6) — Church deactivation or removal from the owning department ends the member's future assignments, flags the vacancies and notifies the leader; a login hold keeps duties and routes contact to the leader; the last leader of an active department and the owner of open tasks are handover obligations; deletion erases or anonymises Duties and Follow-ups personal data. Source: functional-requirements.md Important edge cases (Duties, Follow-up), Access and reliability requirements; AD-14; identity-access.md stories 2.10 and 2.11.
- **D11** (CAP-5, CAP-6) — Owner operations let Cells create, link, revise and cancel cell-owned occurrences and positions through Duties inside Cells' transaction with a unique stable part/position-to-slot link, link an existing department duty without cloning it or transferring its authority, flag an owner-review mismatch when a linked meeting or event changes, and query conflicts. Follow-ups source operations, the event reference and the sermon-draft topic/scripture reference are documented with both-client fixtures for programmes, visitors, care, missing reports/counts and custody. Source: AD-6, AD-7, AD-18; epic Constraints; functional-requirements.md Set up departments and duties (preaching slots).
- **D12** (CAP-5, CAP-6) — A synthetic department pilot exercises publish → remind → explicit response → visible gap → direct contact → confirmation with an app member and an accountless member. After an evidence-led refactor sweep, the implemented workflows reach production once their affected gates pass, with an integrated mobile/staff demonstration and permission/lifecycle regression evidence. Source: delivery-and-decisions.md Success evidence, Milestone 2; AD-18.

## Done when

1. Leaders create draft/published one-off and recurring multi-position department duties with validated ownership, safe overlap warnings and reasoned overrides. One current assignment per slot, occurrence/series exceptions and atomic multi-position edits preserve history and truthful coverage.
2. My duties supports explicit Accept, decline, later decline, revision-specific reconfirmation, selected-date bulk acceptance and separately attributed leader confirmation for direct contact. Stale responses fail; offline/unknown outcomes never appear confirmed; completion/corrections remain separate from response and time passing.
3. Scoped leader queues expose unfilled, unanswered, overdue, declined, material-change and direct-contact needs. Reassignment, cancellation and lifecycle effects update assignment history, coverage, source-linked tasks and notification jobs together.
4. Follow-ups provides one source/purpose-keyed task with accountable owner, supervisor, next action, original due date and current status/review date. All owners see My follow-ups; source-specific minimal context and private notes stay separate; reassignment/resolution removes old-owner or obsolete work without inventing source completion.
5. Permission, revision, replay/concurrency and reminder tests cover app members, accountless members, scope loss and accepted-duty reminders. Shared Duties and Follow-ups owner APIs and fixtures are ready for programmes, visitors, care, missing counts/reports and offering custody; a department pilot exercises the complete response/contact loop.
6. The implemented workflows are deployed to production after their affected operational gates pass, with an integrated mobile/staff demonstration and permission/lifecycle regression evidence for this epic’s own records and actions.

## Boundaries

Owns department setup/standalone duty recurrence, Duties assignments and Follow-ups task/activity records. Does not own cell meeting recurrence, visit consent, attendance or finance facts. Cell programmes call these owner operations instead of cloning their records; Notifications retains inbox/job/attempt ownership.
