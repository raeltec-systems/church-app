# Public church content and giving instructions

## Description

Deliver guest-accessible Bible/hymns, sermons, events, announcements and church-verified giving instructions, with authorized publication, content rights and bounded public caching. Content owns this publishing and the public read contract; Offerings stays a separate private domain.

Milestone: 3.

## Outcome

A visitor can find cleared teaching and event content or follow verified giving instructions without signing in or creating a payer record.

## Requirements

The requirement source is the parent spec, CAP-1 and CAP-2, read with every companion, the architecture and the owner decisions in `owner-decisions-milestone-1.md` and `owner-decisions-milestone-2.md`. The local ids below hold this epic's contribution; each maps to CAP-1 or CAP-2, and some also to CAP-4, CAP-5 or CAP-16, whose permission and cross-client rules each feature applies to its own records. The spec, its companions and the architecture stay authoritative for detail.

- **K1** (CAP-1, CAP-2, CAP-4) — Content publishes through one lifecycle: drafts are private to editors; published revisions are immutable and the current one stays live until a replacement is published; withdraw (hide) and archive are explicit; publish and withdraw are attributed and audited. Media, Pastor and Admin manage church content; only Admin manages giving methods; content roles grant no access to member records, private prayer or giving drafts. Guests, Pending applicants and members read only the current published projection through an unauthenticated public read contract that carries no drafts, editor identity or private fields. Source: functional-requirements.md Roles and permissions, Core data model (Content, Giving), Access and reliability requirements; AD-1, AD-2, AD-4, AD-15.
- **K2** (CAP-1) — Every third-party text or file (Bible translation, hymn source, lyric or translation, recording, image) cites a rights record: holder, basis (public domain, licence, permission or church-owned), cleared uses (in-app display, offline download, audio), evidence, verifier, date and revision. Publishing or offering a download for an uncleared use is refused, and owning a compilation is not permission for each item in it. Production publication of third-party material also needs Israel's Q5 clearance; staging uses public-domain text and SYNTHETIC hymns and media. Source: functional-requirements.md Bible and hymn book, Risks and release checks (Content permissions); delivery-and-decisions.md Q5; AD-13, AD-15.
- **K3** (CAP-1, CAP-4) — Editors upload posters, thumbnails, logos and audio into private draft storage, and the server checks type, size and dimensions. Publishing places a cleared file in a separate public bucket under a revision-keyed path; withdrawal removes it from public paths and projections. Private records and draft files never reach a public path, and both buckets are part of the object backup. Source: AD-13, AD-17; functional-requirements.md Navigation and visual style (posters), Sermons (audio in Storage); design-contract.md Assets.
- **K4** (CAP-1, CAP-2) — The mobile app persists only rights-cleared published public content with its publication and rights revision. It shows when content is saved or offline, refreshes on reconnect, and removes withdrawn or hidden items and their files. Bible and hymn text can be downloaded only where offline use is cleared; giving instructions keep their verification date and an offline label. Protected records never enter this cache, and an already downloaded copy cannot be remotely erased. Source: AD-13; spec-church-app.md Constraints; functional-requirements.md Overview (Constraints), Giving instructions (Administration and safeguards).
- **K5** (CAP-1) — Five tabs (Home, Bible & Hymns, Sermons, Give, Calendar) work without sign-in for guests and Pending applicants. The top bar has the emblem with its accessible church name, search, and the profile or, for guests, Sign in; member-only shortcuts are hidden from guests. My duties, My follow-ups and My cell stay one tap away on Home and in the profile menu. Top-bar search covers published hymns, sermons, events and announcements through the same public projections. Source: functional-requirements.md Navigation and visual style; design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations, State fidelity 9.
- **K6** (CAP-1) — Home opens with a 16:9 snap carousel, the yearly theme first and then campaign and event banners, followed by the guest Join card or the Pending card (separate church-approval and cell-status chips), pinned announcements, Hymns for this Sunday chips from Saturday, and Coming up poster cards for events and sermon series that open the original poster. Images have text alternatives and are cached where the K4 offline rule allows; theme copy is reviewed church content. Source: functional-requirements.md Navigation and visual style (Home content); design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations (Home), Assets.
- **K7** (CAP-1) — The Bible reader has a book and chapter picker, a switch among cleared translations, a chapter heading, numbered verses and previous/next chapter. A cleared translation is loaded through an admin import. Reading plans are out of scope. Source: functional-requirements.md Bible and hymn book; design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations (Bible & Hymns), Tokens, type and geometry (Reading).
- **K8** (CAP-1) — Hymns are searchable by number, title and first line; each has a number, title, verses and chorus, with optional key and audio. Hymns are imported from CSV or plain text, then edited with Media permissions in the staff portal (the source's "in-app" editing), and their text can be downloaded for offline reading where cleared. Source: functional-requirements.md Bible and hymn book; design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations (Bible & Hymns).
- **K9** (CAP-1, CAP-4) — Signed-in approved members save favourite hymns that only they can see. Media or an authorised worship leader sets Hymns for this Sunday for a church-time Sunday; the list shows on Home from Saturday and as a Sunday chip in the hymn list. Source: functional-requirements.md Bible and hymn book; owner-decisions-milestone-2.md (Africa/Lusaka).
- **K10** (CAP-1) — A sermon has a title, speaker, date, series, scripture reference, rich-text notes, a YouTube video id and optional audio. Guests and members browse by Latest, Series and Speaker and search by title; detail embeds the YouTube player; audio streams from Storage with background playback and lock-screen controls. An unavailable video shows a clear message while notes and audio stay available. Media uploads and edits; notes accept only a sanitised subset with allowlisted links. Source: functional-requirements.md Sermons, Important edge cases (Content); design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations (Sermons); AD-15; architecture Deferred (media and rich-text sanitisation).
- **K11** (CAP-1, CAP-5) — A preaching or teaching duty's current assignee or its managers can send its topic and scripture (the 4.11 sermon-draft read) to Content as a sermon draft. Media reviews and completes it; nothing publishes automatically. Source: functional-requirements.md Set up departments and duties; contracts-and-owner-seams.md Duties owner operations (`duties_owner_topic`).
- **K12** (CAP-1) — An event has a title, description, start and end, location, optional poster and optional RSVP with capacity. The Calendar lists events with date badge, thumbnail, start/end and place; detail shows the poster, keeps both endpoints of a multi-day event and offers Add to calendar. Signed-in members RSVP within capacity and can switch on My duties and My cell overlays that show only their own items and open duty detail or My cell. Source: functional-requirements.md Events and announcements, Navigation and visual style (Calendar); design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations (Calendar/event).
- **K13** (CAP-1, CAP-5) — A department duty may name a published event without exposing the rota publicly. A time, place or cancellation change to a linked published event flags the linked duties for their leaders' review through the 4.11 owner operation, which sends one notice per department (owner decision 2026-10-15, item 7); no duty is republished and no response is reset. The editor sees how many duties are linked before saving. Source: functional-requirements.md Events and announcements; AD-6; owner-decisions-milestone-2.md.
- **K14** (CAP-1) — Announcements are short posts that can be pinned. Publication may send a push with generic text and an authorised deep link through the Notifications source contract, in a category members can switch off; the inbox item stays. Guests read announcements in the app. Source: functional-requirements.md Events and announcements; AD-5, AD-8.
- **K15** (CAP-2) — Give opens without sign-in: a How to give explanation that the app neither processes nor records gifts, then the methods (name, optional logo, purpose or currency). Method detail gives individually copyable values, numbered current instructions, an optional dialler handoff with the displayed starting USSD code where supported and the manual steps otherwise, a beneficiary-check reminder, the last verified date and a church contact. Returning to the app shows the instructions again. There is no amount form, payer data, gift record, success state or giving history. Source: functional-requirements.md Giving instructions (Member and guest flow, Method details), Important edge cases (Giving); design-contract.md Mobile screen and flow obligations (Give); AC-07; AD-15.
- **K16** (CAP-2, CAP-4) — Only Admin adds, edits, reorders, publishes, hides and archives giving methods; drafts stay private. Church ownership and accuracy are verified and recorded before first publication and after any destination change. Each revision keeps who changed and published it and when, and the current published revision stays live until a replacement is published. An unsafe flag warns users as soon as a device refreshes. Q3 gates production publication. No payer, gift, proof or transaction entity exists, and Offerings never extends this API. Source: functional-requirements.md Giving instructions (Administration and safeguards), Core data model (Giving), Risks and release checks (Public giving destinations); delivery-and-decisions.md Q3; AD-15.
- **K17** (CAP-1, CAP-2, CAP-4, CAP-16) — Member-linked content records (favourites, RSVPs, editor attribution) follow Identity holds, deactivation and deletion. Media and giving runbooks cover publishing, rights evidence, broken-link checks, unavailable media, destination verification, unsafe methods and support questions. The Guest, Pending, Member, Media, Pastor, Admin and combined-role matrix passes on both clients with chat absent. After a synthetic pilot and an evidence-led refactor sweep, the workflows reach production once their gates pass. Source: functional-requirements.md Membership and account lifecycle, Access and reliability requirements; delivery-and-decisions.md Milestone 3; AD-14, AD-18; AC-07, AC-14.

## Done when

1. Guests can read cleared Bible/hymn text, search hymns, open sermons with available audio/notes and browse events/announcements. Hymn favourites, authorized Sunday lists, content import/edit, media background controls and unavailable-media states follow the baseline.
2. Authorized publishing validates media/rich-text input and rights metadata; public caching/downloads retain publication/rights revision and honest offline/withdrawal states. Private records remain outside those caches and public asset paths.
3. Events support the required calendar export, multi-day endpoints and optional RSVP/capacity behavior. Duty-linked event changes expose a leader-review mismatch; sermon topic/scripture drafts from teaching duties still require Media publication review.
4. How to give provides published numbered instructions, copyable verified details and supported dialler handoff with manual fallback. Admin-only draft/publish/archive and revision history retain the current published revision until replacement, without payment confirmation, payer identity or personal giving records.
5. Guest/member/staff permissions, publication changes, media failures, unsafe input and external handoff/return cases pass on the intended surfaces. Actual giving destinations and distribution rights are verified before publication, and media/giving support procedures are documented.
6. The implemented workflows are deployed to production after their affected operational gates pass, with an integrated mobile/staff demonstration and permission/lifecycle regression evidence for this epic’s own records and actions.

## Boundaries

Content owns public publishing, rights records, public files, the public read contract and instruction-only giving. It reaches Duties only through the 4.11 owner operations (event link source, review flags, the sermon-draft topic read), Notifications only through its source contract, and Identity for grants and lifecycle hooks. Offerings remains a separate private aggregate custody domain; no public Content API may expose or collect donor, collection or payment-result data. Content administration is a staff web surface; on mobile, members only read, save favourites and RSVP. This envelope does not add payments, reading plans, devotionals or livestreaming.
