## Delivery commitment

Israel Muyoba owns and reviews the build, with the pastor's blessing. The target is **full v1, all seven milestones, by November 2026**, not a duty-only pilot. Coding agents implement most changes; one volunteer owner reviews and maintains them in their free time. Owner review capacity, operational decisions and external lead times constrain delivery.

Keep changes small, reviewable and ticket-sized; finish and test each milestone's shared foundations before dependent milestones rely on them. Write RLS/permission, state-transition/concurrency and reminder-worker tests alongside each feature from milestone 1, and run them in CI. Build source-required runbooks for membership/recovery, role/care/custody handover, counts/discrepancies, safe CSV, media links, reminders and database/file restoration. Admin-only support must not acquire restricted care/finance content while executing those procedures.

## Success evidence

The owner wants leaders to stop chasing routine duties, members to know exactly what to do, reminders to arrive on time and members to feel involved. Demonstrate the full publish → remind → explicit response → visible gap → direct follow-up → confirmation loop with both an app member and an accountless member, preserving the latter's distinct leader-recorded provenance. Source launch checks, permissions and failure cases remain release gates rather than an adoption percentage substituting for correctness.

| Measure from the brief | Current status and evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| At least 90% of published duties receive explicit Accept or Can't make it before deadline | Proposed numerical target pending Q11; establish denominator/observation period and a measured baseline. Do not count leader-recorded confirmation as the member's own in-app response. |
| Every slot still unfilled/unconfirmed 48h before service is already in a leader queue | Proposed early-visibility target pending Q11; a queue visibility check, not a new response-deadline rule. Preserve short-notice creation behaviour rather than claiming a not-yet-created slot was visible earlier. |
| Little/no Sunday-morning chasing | Ask leaders after 4–6 weeks; capture their report without inventing a numerical time threshold. |
| No missed/stale reminders in server logs | Verify durable job outcomes, current revisions, retries and expiry; no obsolete reminder is sent. An accepted current duty still receives its scheduled pre-service reminders even though response chasing has ended. Provider acceptance never proves device delivery or reading. Exact on-time tolerance remains to define. |
| Active involvement | Observe share of active members holding at least one duty/programme part per month and repeat use beyond Sunday; no target or baseline was supplied. Define these measures before interpreting results. |

## External lead times and operating constraints

- Configure and test production recovery-email delivery, verified sender/domain and allowed mobile/web reset redirects. No SMS sender registration, Zambia carrier route test or SMS budget is required. Members can omit email and use the approved staff-assisted recovery route; never add a fabricated address.
- Create Apple/Google developer accounts, allow store review including possible rejection of giving instructions, and schedule staff beta/onboarding with the church. Instruction-only giving is not a store-approval guarantee; current Play/iOS, UGC, privacy and deletion requirements need checking before submission.
- Budget hosting, Supabase/backend, storage, production recovery email, store accounts and ongoing Flutter/SDK maintenance using current quotes. No platform subscription does not mean no operating costs.
- WhatsApp habits, varying phone ownership and a single reviewer are adoption/support risks. Preserve assisted no-login participation, fast explicit responses and written support procedures; do not use dated national smartphone statistics as the congregation's baseline.
