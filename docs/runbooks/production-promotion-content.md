## Rollback

6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 6.4, 6.6, 6.9, 6.10, 6.11, 6.13 and 6.18 are additive (no destructive statement, no row deletion; 6.11's `_rows` file deletes only a deleted member's RSVP and organiser rows). To stop RSVPs for an event, an editor turns **Members can RSVP** off and publishes; going RSVPs are kept but no longer counted publicly. To take a banner off Home, an editor withdraws it (or suspends its rights record) and syncs; the public read drops it at once and the next successful mobile refresh removes its local snapshot. To take a giving method off Give at once, an Admin hides it (**Giving** > Hide, `content.hide_giving_method`); to stop all giving publication, leave `q3_giving` unapproved. To take an announcement down, an editor withdraws it (**Announcements** > Withdraw); its undelivered notices are cancelled with it. To take every event off the public calendar without a schema change, an editor withdraws each published event (`content.withdraw_event`, **Events** > Withdraw): the public reads answer without it at once and the next successful mobile refresh removes its local snapshot. To take a translation off the public Bible catalog, an editor withdraws it or removes the rights clearance; already saved offline chapters are not remotely erased. To take a sermon down, withdraw it (a draft sent from a duty is never public until published; archive an unwanted one); the next successful canonical-catalog refresh evicts its saved row. Withdrawing a series removes only its public title/poster, and a file sync removes the public poster object. Removing video clearance omits the playable ID (or Facebook link, 6.10) while retaining the teaching. Already saved offline content cannot be remotely erased. To stop someone editing, an Admin removes their Media, Pastor or Admin role (**Roles & access**); it applies to their next command. No down migration is needed.
